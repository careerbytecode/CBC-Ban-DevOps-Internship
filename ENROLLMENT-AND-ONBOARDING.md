## ☁️ Cloud-cost note — please read before you enroll

This is a **hands-on cloud program**. From **Week 3** and during your **capstone**, you use **your own Microsoft Azure account**, and the labs create **real, billable cloud resources** (Virtual Machines, AKS clusters, Application Gateway, Container Registry).

- 💳 **Expect small charges** for the time your lab resources are running. This is **separate from the program fee** and is paid directly to Microsoft Azure by you.
- 🆓 **Use the free tier / free credits.** New Azure accounts usually include free starting credits — mentors will help you make the most of them.
- 🧹 **Always clean up.** Delete or stop your resources after each lab (e.g. `az group delete --name <your-rg> --yes --no-wait`). This is the single biggest way to keep your costs minimal.
- 🧪 You can practise many Week 1–2 steps **locally for free** (Docker Desktop + a local cluster) before moving to Azure.

> We keep this transparent on purpose: the small cloud spend is part of learning real-world DevOps, and the skills (and portfolio) you gain are well worth it.

## 🔁 Refund & completion policy

- **Before the cohort starts:** full refund if you withdraw before Day 0 of your cohort.
- **After Week 1 has begun:** the program is live and seats are limited, so fees are generally non-refundable once training has started — talk to your mentors if you have a genuine difficulty and we'll do our best to accommodate (e.g., moving you to a later cohort).
- **Cloud charges** are billed by Azure to you directly and are **not** refundable by CareerByteCode.
- **Certificate** is issued on completion of the criteria in [`CERTIFICATE-CRITERIA.md`](CERTIFICATE-CRITERIA.md), not simply on payment.

## 🤝 What we honestly promise (and what we don't)

We want you to enroll with clear expectations:

- ✅ **We do provide:** live mentor-led Cloud & DevOps training, hands-on labs, a real DevOps project you build and own, a public portfolio, and a **verifiable certificate** with a reference ID.
- ✅ **We do open doors:** membership in a 2.4M+ global tech community, plus visibility to people in our network through the work you publish.
- ⚠️ **We do not guarantee a job, an internship stipend, or any specific income.** The certificate is a genuine credential of completion — it is **not** a government or university qualification, and it does not by itself confer immigration points or guarantee employment. What gets you hired is the skill and the public portfolio you build here.
