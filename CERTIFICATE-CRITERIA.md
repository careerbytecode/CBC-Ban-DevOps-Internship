# 🎓 Certificate Criteria

On completing this internship you receive a **verifiable Internship Certificate from CareerByteCode**, issued with a **unique reference ID**.

You can:
- ✅ Add it to your **CV** and **LinkedIn** (Licenses & Certifications)
- ✅ Show **employers** verifiable proof of a completed, real DevOps project
- ✅ Use it as evidence of practical, hands-on Cloud & DevOps experience

> ℹ️ **What the certificate is — honestly.** It is an official, verifiable certificate of *completion* issued by CareerByteCode with a unique reference ID, and a strong portfolio credential. It is **not** a government or university qualification and does **not** by itself confer immigration points or guarantee a job. What employers value is the real project and public portfolio behind it.

---

## ✅ What you must complete (4-week program)

All of the following must be done and **verified by your mentors**:

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | **Attendance** — attend the Weeks 1–3 live classes (catch up via recordings if you miss one, with mentor's note) |
| 2 | **Profile** — internship added to your LinkedIn profile (Experience) + "I've joined" post |
| 3 | **Weekly posts** — LinkedIn posts for **Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, and the Capstone** (5 posts total incl. the joining post), each tagging **@CareerByteCode + all 7 mentors** |
| 4 | **Weekly videos** — one short video per week on **LinkedIn**, and on **Instagram** with **`careerbytecode` as collaborator** |
| 5 | **DevOps capstone** — one DevOps project (your assigned project): app Dockerized, deployed to **AKS** via an **Azure CI/CD pipeline**, with **monitoring** (Azure Monitor + Container Insights). Code pushed to `interns/Your-Name/ProjectXX-[DEVOPS]-Name/` with a complete `README.md`, manifests, pipeline, screenshots, and a 3–5 min demo video |
| 6 | **Registry** — your capstone is listed in [`PROJECTS-CATALOG.md`](PROJECTS-CATALOG.md) (Cohort Capstone Registry) |
| 7 | **Demo Day** — present your capstone live (demo + Q&A) |
| 8 | **Submissions log** — all post/video links recorded in your `SUBMISSIONS.md` and verified in the master tracker |

> Your certificate is issued on completion of **all** of the above — not on payment alone.

### ⏱️ Your certificate shows your live mentor hours
Your certificate states your **total live mentor-led training hours** and **number of classes**, drawn from the [`MENTOR-HOURS.md`](tracking/MENTOR-HOURS.md) log — a true, verifiable record of the contact time behind your training.

### 🏆 Top-intern pathway
The most active interns each cohort top the [`LEADERBOARD.md`](LEADERBOARD.md) and enter the [`HALL-OF-FAME.md`](HALL-OF-FAME.md). Standout interns are **invited to grow as CareerByteCode community mentors** and **offered access to CareerByteCode freelancing opportunities** — an invitation earned through consistent work, not a guaranteed placement.
