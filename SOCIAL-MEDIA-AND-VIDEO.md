## 1️⃣ The weekly LinkedIn post
- Write about what you learned / built that week (use the script in [`WEEKLY-SCRIPTS.md`](WEEKLY-SCRIPTS.md)).
- Tag **@CareerByteCode + all 7 mentors**.
- Add a relevant image (a screenshot of your terminal, your running pods, your pipeline, or a photo of you).
- For the **Week 4** post, include your **GitHub project link**.

---

## 2️⃣ The weekly video
A short **60–90 second** self-speech video about that week's learning. It builds confidence, communication, and your personal brand. (Your **Week-4 capstone demo video is longer — 3–5 minutes** — to properly show your app running on AKS, the pipeline, and the monitoring dashboard.)

**Set-up tips:**
- Use a teleprompter app so you can read naturally. **Prompter Pal** (Android): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.solid.teleprompter
- Film in landscape or portrait, good light, quiet room. Keep weekly videos to ~60–90s.
- Use the matching video script in [`WEEKLY-SCRIPTS.md`](WEEKLY-SCRIPTS.md).

---

## 3️⃣ Post the video on LinkedIn **and** Instagram

**LinkedIn:** post the video, tag **@CareerByteCode + all 7 mentors**, same hashtags.

**Instagram:** post the video as a Reel/post and **add `careerbytecode` as a Collaborator**:
1. Create the Reel/post with your video.
2. On the final screen → **Tag people** → **Invite collaborator**.
3. Search **`careerbytecode`** and add it as a **collaborator**.
4. Share.

> 💡 Adding us as a collaborator sends an invite to the CareerByteCode Instagram account — that's how we verify it, and once accepted your video also appears on the official CBC profile. No invite = it doesn't count.
