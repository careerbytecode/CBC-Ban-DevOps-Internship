# 🌍 About CareerByteCode

**CareerByteCode is a global platform for tech professionals** to learn production-grade skills, build real-world projects, collaborate with experts, and grow their careers — all in one ecosystem spanning **2.4M+ professionals across 105+ countries**.

We don't believe in passive learning. We believe in:

| Principle | Our Approach |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Execution over Certificates** | Build real systems, not just collect badges |
| **Projects over Theory** | Every concept is taught through real use cases |
| **Mentorship over Isolation** | Learn alongside a global community |
| **Build in Public over Building in Silence** | Share your work so the world can see your growth |
